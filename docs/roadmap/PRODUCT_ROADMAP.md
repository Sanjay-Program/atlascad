## 1. Vision Statement

To establish **AtlasCAD** as the undisputed, world-leading unified engineering, simulation, design, and digital manufacturing platform. AtlasCAD eliminates fragmented software silos by providing an integrated, memory-safe, hardware-accelerated workstation running on a single continuous engineering data model.

## 2. Multi-Horizon Release Roadmap

```
Horizon 1 (Current v2.0 - Foundation & Multi-Discipline Integration)
├── 3D B-Rep Modeling (Truck) & AutoCAD-Grade 2D Drafting (Paper Space Layouts)
├── 11 Analytical Physics Solvers (CFD, FEA, Thermal, Modal, Aero, Chem, Robotics, etc.)
├── Advanced Subtractive CAM (4-Axis Rotary & 5-Axis Multi-Axis Normal Contouring)
├── Additive Manufacturing Platform (Adaptive Layer Height & TPMS Gyroid Infill)
└── Enterprise Governance ($10,000/mo SaaS tier, Cryptographic Audit, AS9100/ISO 9001)

Horizon 2 (v2.5 - High-Performance Scalability & Cloud Worker Federation)
├── GPU-Accelerated WebGPU Render Pipeline & Large Assembly Culling (> 50,000 components)
├── Distributed Slurm / Kubernetes HPC Simulation Worker Clusters
├── Advanced Composite Material Lamina & Ply Layup Damage Simulation
└── Automated 3D Multi-Body Collision Avoidance for Robotic Workcells

Horizon 3 (v3.0 - Autonomous Generative Engineering & Digital Twins)
├── Multi-Physics Co-Simulation (Coupled Fluid-Structure Interaction with preCICE)
├── Neural Surrogate Accelerated Topology Optimization (Sub-second physics inference)
├── IoT Live Digital Twin Ingestion with Real-Time Stress & Fatigue Damage Accrual
└── Direct Machine Protocol Interfaces (MTConnect / OPC-UA for closed-loop CNC compensation)
```

## 3. Horizon 1 Detailed Milestone Deliverables (Achieved)

- **CAD/Drafting Core:** B-Rep solids, CSG parametric timeline, dynamic rollback, multi-sheet vector PDF and DXF plotting.
- **Multiphysics Validation:** 11 analytical solvers validated against theoretical mechanics with error $< 1\%$.
- **Subtractive CAM:** Modal $(I, J, K)$ multi-axis G-code, heightmap stock simulation, tool holder clearance verification, live 3D toolpath lines.
- **Additive Manufacturing:** Cross-sectional polygon contouring, Dolenc & Mäkelä adaptive layer height, volumetric extrusion kinematics, live 3D layer scrubber.
- **Enterprise SaaS:** 128-bit FNV-1a hash chain, 3-tier digital sign-offs, priority HPC job queue, formal ECO lifecycle.
